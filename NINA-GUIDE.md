## 1. Your Site at a Glance

Your website is live at **[nintron.com](https://nintron.com)**.

Here's what it has:

- **Gallery** — all your paintings, each with its own page
- **Journal** — your writing, thoughts, and process posts
- **About** — your bio and artist statement

The site is built to last. It's fast, it works on phones and computers, and it doesn't cost anything to run month to month. Your domain (nintron.com) is the only annual cost — about $20/year through GoDaddy.

Nobody can accidentally break anything from the outside. The only way to change the site is to log into your dashboard, which only you have access to.

## 2. Logging Into Your Dashboard

Your dashboard is where you manage everything — adding paintings, writing posts, updating your bio.

**To log in:**
1. Go to **[nintron.com/admin](https://nintron.com/admin)**
2. Click the login button
3. You'll be taken to GitHub to authorize — click **Authorize**
4. You'll be brought straight into your dashboard

That's it. No password to remember, no invite email. It uses your GitHub account to verify who you are.

**You will need a free GitHub account to log in.**
If you don't have one yet, go to [github.com](https://github.com) and sign up for free — it only takes a few minutes. Once you have one, let your developer know your GitHub username so they can give you access.

**If you get logged out:**
Just go back to nintron.com/admin and click the login button again. It'll reconnect through GitHub automatically.

**Bookmark this link:** nintron.com/admin

## 3. Adding a New Painting to the Gallery

1. Log into your dashboard at nintron.com/admin
2. Click **Gallery** in the left sidebar
3. Click the **New Gallery** button in the top right
4. Fill in the fields:
   - **Title** — the name of the piece
   - **Medium** — e.g. "Acrylic on canvas" or "Colored pencil on paper"
   - **Year** — the year you made it
   - **Publish Date** — today's date is fine
   - **SEO Image → Image** — click this to upload your photo
5. Scroll down to the body area if you want to add a description
6. When you're ready, click **Publish** in the upper right

The painting will appear in your Gallery within about 60 seconds.

**Tips for photos:**
- Take the photo in good natural light
- Shoot straight on, not at an angle
- JPEG files work great — no need to resize, the site handles that automatically

## 4. Writing a Journal Post

1. Log into your dashboard at nintron.com/admin
2. Click **Journal** in the left sidebar
3. Click **New Journal** in the top right
4. Fill in:
   - **Title** — the title of your post
   - **Excerpt** — one sentence that summarizes it (shows as a preview on the Journal page)
   - **Publish Date** — the date you want it to show
   - **Body** — your writing goes here. You can write in plain text, bold things, add links, etc.
5. Click **Publish** when ready

Your post will appear on the Journal page within about 60 seconds.

**You don't need to write perfectly formatted text.** Just write naturally. The site handles all the formatting for you.

## 5. Editing Your About Page

1. Log into your dashboard at nintron.com/admin
2. Click **Pages** in the left sidebar
3. Click **About**
4. Edit the text in the body area — update your bio, tweak your artist statement, whatever feels right
5. Click **Publish** to save

Your About page currently has a placeholder bio based on general information. **Update this with your own words when you're ready** — especially the part about where you grew up and where you've lived.

## 6. Updating Your Contact Info

Your contact page currently has a placeholder email address. Here's how to update it:

1. Log into your dashboard at nintron.com/admin
2. Click **Pages** → **Get in Touch**
3. Find the line that says `your@email.com`
4. Replace it with your real email address
5. Click **Publish**

This is important — it's how people get in touch with you about commissions, prints, and everything else.

## 7. Renaming a Painting

All the painting titles in the Gallery are working titles chosen during the build. If you want to rename any of them:

1. Log into your dashboard at nintron.com/admin
2. Click **Gallery**
3. Click the painting you want to rename
4. Change the **Title** field
5. Click **Publish**

The URL of that painting's page will also update automatically.

## 8. What NOT to Touch

A few things in the dashboard that are best left alone unless you know what you're doing:

- **The slug field** (if you see one) — this is the URL of the page. Changing it can break links.
- **Deleting a page** — you can unpublish things, but avoid deleting the About or Contact pages entirely
- **The image alt text fields** — these are fine to leave as-is

If you're ever unsure about something, just don't click Publish yet — close the tab and ask for help first. Nothing is permanent until you hit Publish.

---

## 9. Who to Call When Something Looks Wrong

If something on your site looks broken or doesn't look right, don't panic — the site itself is very stable.

Before reaching out, check:
- Is it just your browser? Try opening the site in a different browser or on your phone
- Did you recently publish something? Sometimes it takes a minute or two to update

When you do reach out, it helps to have:
- The URL of the page that looks wrong
- A screenshot if possible
- What you were trying to do when it happened
