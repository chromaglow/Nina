## 1. Your Site at a Glance

## 2. Logging Into Your Dashboard

Your dashboard is where you manage everything — adding paintings, writing posts, updating your bio.

To log in, you need a free **GitHub account**. GitHub is a website that stores your site's files — think of it like a filing cabinet in the cloud. You don't need to understand it or use it for anything else. You just need an account so the system can verify who you are.

---

### Step A — Create a free GitHub account (first time only)

1. Open a web browser and go to **[github.com](https://github.com)**
2. Click the **Sign up** button in the top right corner
3. Enter your email address and click **Continue**
4. Create a password and click **Continue**
5. Choose a username — something simple like `ninartist` or your name — and click **Continue**
6. Follow the remaining steps to verify your email and finish setup
7. Once your account is created, **send your GitHub username to your developer** so they can give you access to your dashboard

You only do this once. After that, your GitHub account is your key to your dashboard forever.

---

### Step B — Log into your dashboard

1. Open a web browser and go to **[nintron.com/admin](https://nintron.com/admin)**
2. You'll see a page with a login button — click it
3. You'll be taken to GitHub — if you're already signed into GitHub it will happen automatically; if not, it will ask for your GitHub email and password
4. Click **Authorize** when GitHub asks for permission
5. You'll land straight inside your dashboard — you're in

No separate password to remember. Your GitHub account is the key.

---

### If you get logged out

Just go back to **nintron.com/admin** and click the login button again. It reconnects through GitHub automatically.

**Bookmark this page:** nintron.com/admin

## 4. Adding a New Painting to the Gallery

1. Log into your dashboard at nintron.com/admin
2. Click **Gallery** in the left sidebar
3. Click the **New Gallery** button in the top right corner
4. Fill in the fields:
   - **TITLE** — the name of the piece *(required)*
   - **MEDIUM (OPTIONAL)** — e.g. "Acrylic on canvas" or "Colored pencil on paper"
   - **YEAR (OPTIONAL)** — the year you made it
   - **PUBLISH DATE** — click the **Now** button to set it to today *(required)*
   - **FEATURED** — toggle this on if you want this piece highlighted on your homepage
   - **SEO IMAGE (OPTIONAL)** — click the arrow ▼ to expand this section, then expand **IMAGE (OPTIONAL)** and click to upload your photo. This is the image that appears in the Gallery grid.
   - **DESCRIPTION (OPTIONAL)** — scroll down to write anything about the piece
5. When everything looks good, click the teal **Published ▼** button in the top bar
6. Click the **▼** arrow on the button and make sure **Published** is selected

The painting will appear in your Gallery within about 60 seconds. You can click **View Live ↗** in the top bar to see it on your site.

**Tips for photos:**
- Take the photo in good natural light
- Shoot straight on, not at an angle
- JPEG files work great — no need to resize, the site handles that automatically

## 5. Writing a Journal Post

1. Log into your dashboard at nintron.com/admin
2. Click **Journal** in the left sidebar
3. Click the **New Journal** button in the top right corner
4. Fill in the fields:
   - **TITLE** — the title of your post *(required)*
   - **EXCERPT (OPTIONAL)** — one sentence that summarizes it. This is what people see as a preview on your Journal page before they click to read the full post
   - **PUBLISH DATE** — click the **Now** button to set it to today *(required)*
   - **FEATURED** — toggle on if you want this post highlighted on your homepage
   - **TAGS (OPTIONAL)** — words that describe the post, like "process" or "new work". You can skip this.
   - **BODY** — your writing goes here *(required)*. Use the formatting toolbar above the text area to make text **bold**, *italic*, add headings, or insert images
5. When you're ready, click the teal **Published ▼** button in the top bar and make sure **Published** is selected

Your post will appear on the Journal page within about 60 seconds.

**You don't need to write perfectly formatted text.** Just write naturally and use the formatting toolbar if you want to style anything.

## 6. Editing Your About Page

1. Log into your dashboard at nintron.com/admin
2. Click **Pages** in the left sidebar
3. Click **About**
4. You'll see two fields:
   - **TITLE** — the page title (you can leave this as "About")

5. When you're done, click the teal **Published** button in the top bar to save

Your changes will appear on the live site within about 60 seconds. Click **View Live ↗** to check it.

## 7. Updating Your Contact Info

Your contact page currently has a placeholder email address. Here's how to update it:

1. Log into your dashboard at nintron.com/admin
2. Click **Pages** in the left sidebar
3. Click **Get in Touch**
4. Click inside the **BODY** field
5. Find the line that says `your@email.com` and replace it with your real email address
6. Click the teal **Published** button to save

This is important — it's how people reach you about commissions, prints, and everything else.

## 9. Saving and Publishing — How It Works

Whenever you make a change, here's what to know:

- You'll see **CHANGES SAVED** in green text near the top — this means your changes are saved as a draft but not yet live
- To make something go live, click the teal **Published ▼** button and confirm **Published** is selected
- Once published, your change appears on the live site within about 60 seconds
- You can click **View Live ↗** in the top bar at any time to see your live site

**The difference between Gallery/Journal and Pages:**
- Gallery and Journal entries have a **Published ▼** button with a dropdown arrow — you can set them to Draft, Pending Review, or Published
- Pages (About, Get in Touch) have a **Published** button with no dropdown — they are always published when you save

## 10. What NOT to Touch

A few things in the dashboard that are best left alone:

- **Delete entry** — the red button in the top bar. Avoid clicking this unless you really mean to permanently remove something. There is no undo.
- **The Terms of Service page** — you'll see it listed under Pages. Just ignore it, it won't show on your site.
- **The slug field** (if you ever see one) — this is the behind-the-scenes URL of a page. Changing it can break links.

If you're ever unsure about something, just close the tab without clicking **Published**. Nothing goes live until you hit that button.

---

## 11. Who to Call When Something Looks Wrong

If something on your site looks broken or doesn't look right, don't panic — the site itself is very stable.

Before reaching out, check:
- Is it just your browser? Try opening the site in a different browser or on your phone
- Did you recently publish something? Sometimes it takes a minute or two to update

When you do reach out, it helps to have:
- The URL of the page that looks wrong
- A screenshot if possible
- What you were trying to do when it happened
