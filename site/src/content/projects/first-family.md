    alt: "This art piece concept was a vision of Dexy Valentine from Magic Wands, I
      was just the Chanel for this creative vision, based on Magic Wands and
      John Ashton’s song “Sacred Mirror.   Today, the song “Sacred Mirrors” By
      Magic Wands has been released on Metropolis Records. I am particularly
      excited about the release as it has been a deep reflection of my own
      personal journey throw heartbreak back to love.  As my heart shatters,
      this is a great reminder to keep on dreaming and never grow bitter.  This
      song has been a very meta experience for me to unpack a song that has
      strong ties to my own experience.  This song has so much depth. I’ve been
      listening to this every day since I was sent the ruff mix, and every day I
      find another layer to unpack.  I couldn’t be more honored to have my own
      personal experience of heartbreak to be one of the insertions for this
      song.  So here it goes:    “Sacred Mirrors” is a Cosmic love song about
      two outsiders who find belonging in each other.  Their connection is so
      strong that it becomes a sacred mirror, revealing their truest selves,
      seeing each other for their full potential, and giving them the courage to
      dream beyond fear, to the spiritual realm where they can be together
      always. Through moonlit, infinite imagery, love becomes spiritual
      awakening, an invitation to grow, to trust the unknown, and to share the
      most intimate, vulnerable experience: dreaming together.   This song
      heavily reminds me of Alan Watts Philosophy on Dreams and Love.  To “dream
      everything there could be”, brings us back to this very moment.  You have
      to dream big and believe in yourself to achieve your goals and
      aspirations.  To make things a reality, you have to dream it first.  Watts
      believed Love is an energy we possess; to love oneself is to let that
      energy flow. When we deny the flow of loving energy, we become bitter and
      destructive.  How does one continue to let the energy flow when they are
      hurt, so they do not grow bitter and dead inside?   Forever and always
      does not exist on this plane; it exist in the spiritual realm. Dreams
      allow us to enter the spiritual realm.  Dreams are acts of love that
      awaken us to the Spiritual connection we have to ourselves and others. To
      be a lover, you must be a dreamer. And to be a dreamer, you must be a
      lover.   Forever and always can also be interpreted as the self and
      soul.  We are with ourselves always. The sacred Mirror was merely a
      reflection or projection of themselves in Jungian terms.  The mirror
      activated something in ourselves. To see what love is and a deeper
      connection to the unconscious, so they could integrate these lessons into
      themselves and allow for individualization.  This is also a self-love
      song.  The artist no longer needs someone else to carry those
      traits.   This song, in all its beauty, is a reminder to me in my darkness
      to always keep dreaming. I am love, and that I will still let it flow even
      in my pain.   That love is still all around me, even in our heartbreak,
      because when we turn away from love, we stop dreaming and growing.  We
      grow bitter and destructive.      “I want to dream everything it could
      be.”"
